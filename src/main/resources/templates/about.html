About HamroKitab

Every book deserves another chapter.

HamroKitab is a student-focused platform for buying and selling second-hand academic books. We make it easier to find affordable study materials while helping useful books move from one student to another.

Who We Are

A simple place for students and books.

Academic books can be expensive, and many are used for only a semester or two. HamroKitab provides a simple way for students to find books they need and sell books they no longer use.

Instead of letting useful books sit unused, HamroKitab connects students who have books with students who are looking for them.

Whether someone is looking for a textbook, entrance preparation material, or a book for a particular subject, the platform is designed to make that search simpler.

Our Mission

Making academic books easier to access.

Our goal is to create an easy and student-friendly platform where academic books can be reused, shared and exchanged. HamroKitab focuses on making second-hand books easier to discover while supporting affordable learning.
